Rules

Rules for navigation

What goes in the sidebar

  • Places, never actions and never single records. Every row opens a page that follows an archetype (list, queue, settings list, home).
  • Two groups named after the work: Daily work (Home, Trucks, Trips, Maintenance, Money) and Company (Personnel, Branches, Accounting months). Money is one row (see Money).
  • Hidden when the module is off or the role can't read it. Never greyed, never a link to a denial.
  • The row label is the page title, word for word, and the first breadcrumb.
  • Personal things (language, theme, sign out) live in the name menu at the bottom. The "More" page goes away.

How it behaves

  • Counts only where someone has to act, from server aggregates: expenses waiting for your approval (on Money), new Maintenance problems. Red means it waits on you.
  • One active-route matcher (isRouteActive), active on child routes too.
  • No nested, collapsible groups: one click to any place.
  • Collapsed rail on desktop: icons, tooltips, counts become dots.
  • Second-level navigation inside a page is always URL tabs of one style (the vehicle's line tabs, 44 px).
  • Visibility is data: sections.ts lists group, module and the read capability, so a test can assert each role's sidebar.
Money

Money: three pages today, one place proposed

Today Finance has three pages behind pill tabs: Entries, Approvals and Periods. They are three views of one flow, not three separate things.

1 · RecordedAnyone allowed records an expense or revenue: on a truck, a trip, when completing a work order, or in Money. It becomes an entry with status "Waiting".
→
2 · Approved or rejectedA finance approver or admin decides. Approvals is just the list of entries still "Waiting", oldest first, across their branches.
→
3 · PostedApproved entries count in the books for the month of their date. Mistakes are reversed, never edited.
→
4 · Month lockedAt month end, finance locks the period (the accounting month). Nothing posts into it afterwards; late corrections go into the open month.

The "Waiting your approval" tile is the approvals queue: clicking it filters the list and shows Approve / Reject on each row. The count on the Money row in the sidebar opens straight into this view.

The simplification

  • Entries + Approvals → one Money page. Approvals becomes the "Waiting approval" view of the same list. Decision buttons appear on rows only for people who can approve.
  • Periods leave daily navigation. The Money page's lead line says which month is open. Locking and reopening move to Company → Accounting months, for finance approvers and admins only.
  • Old links keep working: /finance/approvals redirects to the waiting view; notifications link there.
  • The in-page pill tabs (one of the four tab styles) go away.

Case for keeping three

  • Approvers get a page that is only their queue, nothing else on it.
  • No routing changes, no redirects.

Why one still wins

  • The filtered view is that queue: one click from the sidebar count, with rows people already know.
  • Periods are a monthly admin task; a daily-navigation spot makes everyone else walk past it.
  • Fewer places to learn: a driver's expense, the approver's queue and the ledger are the same list.
