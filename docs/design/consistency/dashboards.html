Dashboards

Every page opens the way a truck's page already does: an Overview tab first, then the tabs where the work happens. The Overview is that page's dashboard: how things are going, why, and what needs you. Home is the company Dashboard. Money words are kept to the ones everyone uses: revenue, expenses, profit.

The header (title, one sentence, the one main button) stays the same on every tab; only the area under the tabs changes.

How tabs behave

  • The page opens on Overview. The tab is in the address, so a link or the back button lands on the same tab.
  • A tile on the Overview opens its tab with the filter already set ("Missing receipt 4" opens Entries filtered).
  • A count on a tab only where someone waits (To approve 6, Problems 2).
  • Tabs a role can't use are hidden, never greyed.

What an Overview holds

  • A summary notice: one sentence that shows on a schedule and can be closed. What it counted stays as one line under the tiles.
  • 4–5 tiles, compared with the same days last month.
  • One or two charts that explain the change.
  • "To do": up to 5 things, ranked by money or safety, one button each.
  • Every role gets an Overview; what's in it follows the role.

What changes from today

  • The tiles at the top of each list move into the Overview; the list tab starts with its search and filters.
  • "Waiting approval" becomes the Money tab "To approve" (same list, saved filter).
  • Settings pages (Branches, Categories, Company settings) keep one list and no tabs: there's nothing to oversee.
  • The "Module list" archetype becomes "Module page" in README.md.

The summary is a notice, not a fixture

When it shows

  • On the schedule the company picks: by default every Monday morning and on the 1st of the month (last month's result).
  • It also shows when something big changes: a truck grounded, spending 20% above last month.
  • Each person closes it for themselves. It stays closed until the next one.

When it's closed

  • The Overview starts with the tiles.
  • The "what was counted" line moves under the tiles, so the warning about missing data never disappears.
  • "Show summary" in that line brings it back.
  • Same behaviour on every Overview tab (Money, Trucks, Trips…), on one schedule.

Company settings › Dashboard summary

Administrators set it. Each person can still close it.

Why company costs stay separate

Office rent isn't split across trucks. Splitting it would make every truck look worse in a way nobody on the road can fix. So each truck's profit shows what that truck earned and spent, and the office costs appear once, as their own line.

Words we stop using

  • Contribution → profit (it meant truck profit before office costs)
  • Margin, marge → profit, with "20% of revenue" beside it
  • Money in / money out, Entrées / Sorties → revenue / expenses. The French Money tiles say "Sorties" while the English ones already say "Expenses"; both become the same words.

What profit in ROUTIQ is not

It's the profit from what was recorded in ROUTIQ. It doesn't include taxes, loan repayments or the truck losing value over the years. Every Overview says so in one line under the summary, so nobody takes it for the accountant's figure.

Companies with no revenue

An internal fleet (a company running trucks for its own work) records no revenue, so profit means nothing there. Its Overviews show expenses, cost per km, cost per truck per day and how many trucks can work. Profit appears only when the company records revenue.

Summary sentence rules

  • Each page has 4–8 templates: ahead, behind, flat, no data yet, first month.
  • The named reason is the biggest bar in the bridge or the biggest change by category.
  • Before the 3rd of the month, it talks about last month instead.
  • If fewer than half the month's trips are closed, it says so and gives no profit.
  • Whole sentences in fr-CM and en; no stitched phrases.

Comparison rules

  • This month is compared with the same days of last month (Oct 1–9 vs Sep 1–9).
  • Colour follows meaning: expenses up is red, revenue up is green, always with ▲▼ so colour is never alone.
  • Small changes show the amount, not a percent ("+20 000").
  • Whole numbers, space between thousands. The currency label (FCFA or XAF) is decision 9; these mockups use XAF until it's picked.
