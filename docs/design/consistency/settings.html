Company settings

Each company can make ROUTIQ feel like its own: its logo, its colour, its defaults and working rules. The Administrator sets them once and they apply to everyone in the company. ROUTIQ stays the product: the "powered by ROUTIQ" mark, the app icon and the login footer never change. Each person also keeps their own small set of preferences.

Placement

Where settings live

Company settings

Sidebar → Company → Company settings. Administrator and Direction only. Changes apply to every user of the company, on every device, at their next page load.

My settings

The name menu at the bottom of the sidebar → My settings. Every user: language, light / dark, PIN, signed-in devices. Only affects that person.

Not here

Modules and the plan are set by ROUTIQ in the console; the company sees them read-only under Company → Your modules.

What the company can change

  • Logo (sidebar header, sign-in page, printed receipts and statements).
  • Accent colour (from a checked set, or a custom colour that passes contrast).
  • Sidebar light or dark.
  • Company name as displayed.

What always stays ROUTIQ

  • "powered by ROUTIQ" with the mark, at the bottom of the sidebar and on the sign-in page.
  • The app icon and the browser tab icon.
  • Status colours, typography, layout, the design system. Personalising never changes how the app works, only how it's dressed.
  • The console (console.routiq.app) is always ROUTIQ-branded.

Build

Rules and what it takes

Rules

  • Company settings are a core feature (every company has them); module settings appear inside them only when the module is on.
  • Every change is a command, audited, visible in History ("Esther changed the accent colour").
  • Theme is limited to accent colour and sidebar shade on purpose: a fully free theme would break contrast, status colours and the consistency this proposal is about.
  • The logo is stored through the existing S3 storage (uploads), never in the database.

What it takes

  • A workspace_settings record + update-company-settings and upload-company-logo commands.
  • The shell reads the settings at sign-in and sets the accent as CSS variables; tokens already drive every colour (palette guard), so no screen changes.
  • Screens for the two backend-only features: Approvals and Categories.
  • Move language to the account (#127).
