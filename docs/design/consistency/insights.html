Overviews and charts

Every page opens with an overview. Most overviews are numbers that filter the list below. Charts are added only where the question is "how is this changing?", and that question belongs mostly to executives and administrators. This page marks every spot where a chart goes, what question it answers, and who sees it.

The rule

When a chart, when a number

Number (stat tile)

"How much right now?" Grounded trucks, waiting approvals, cost this month. Every role sees these at the top of every list. Each tile filters the list under it.

Number + sparkline + change

"Is it going up or down?" The same tile with last month's change ("▲ 18% vs September") and a small trend line. Shown to managers, finance and executives.

Chart

"Why, where, and since when?" Cost by month, by branch, by truck; availability over time. Only on the executive overview, the module overviews for managers, and a truck's Money tab. Each chart answers one question named in its subtitle and links to the list behind it.

Phone rules

  • Four tiles in a 2 × 2 grid, each with change and sparkline. The fifth tile drops off on phone.
  • One chart (cost per month), full width. The other charts become short ranked lists, which read better than tiny bars at 390 px.
  • Tapping a tile opens its list, filtered.
  • No hover on phone: tapping a bar shows its value under the chart.

Rules

Chart rules

What a chart may be

  • Trend over time: line (single series as area or line), months or weeks on the x-axis.
  • Comparing amounts: horizontal bars, sorted largest first, one colour.
  • Two measures with different units (cost and km): two charts, never two y-axes.
  • No pies, no donuts, no gauges, no 3D. A single number is a tile, not a chart.
  • Up to 3 coloured series per chart; past that, "Other" or separate small charts.

How it looks and behaves

  • Thin marks: 2 px lines, bars with 4 px rounded ends and 2 px gaps; light grid, muted axis text.
  • Series colours: blue, then orange, then aqua (checked for colour blindness). Red, amber and green stay reserved for status.
  • Two or more series: a legend plus a label at the end of each line. Text stays in text colours, never the series colour.
  • Hover (desktop) or tap (phone) shows exact values. Every chart has a "Table" view.
  • The subtitle states the question and the unit. The footer links to the list behind the chart.
  • Money in XAF, integers, space as thousands separator, tabular figures. Partial periods labelled ("October, 4 days").
  • Numbers come from server summaries for the chosen branch and range, never counted in the browser.
  • Neutral theme note: develop's chart tokens are five greys. Charts need these three hues added as chart tokens; the rest of the UI stays neutral.
